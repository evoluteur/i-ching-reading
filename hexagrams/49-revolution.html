Revolution (革, Gé) is hexagram 49 of the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching, in the traditional King Wen order. It is made of Lake (☱) above Fire (☲).

Meaning of hexagram 49

The old order has outlived its use and change is due. Radical change succeeds when the time is right and the cause is fair.

Advice

Prepare carefully, win trust before you act and change decisively.

The two trigrams

Revolution places ☱ Lake (Duì), joyful, open and pleasing, above ☲ Fire (Lí), bright, clinging and clear. The lower trigram is the inner side of the situation and the upper one its outer side.

The six lines of Revolution

A hexagram is read from the bottom line up. In a reading, a changing line (a 6 or a 9 in the three-coin method) draws attention to its position.

  1. First line (yang, solid): the beginning. The root of the matter: early stirrings, foundations, and what has not yet shown itself.
  2. Second line (yin, broken): the inner circle. Your immediate surroundings and steady supports: how things work at close range.
  3. Third line (yang, solid): the threshold. A point of tension between inner and outer, often a test or a restless push forward.
  4. Fourth line (yang, solid): the approach. Stepping into wider circles or nearer to authority; caution and good relationships matter here.
  5. Fifth line (yang, solid): the center of power. The place of influence and clear leadership: where the situation is most fully expressed.
  6. Sixth line (yin, broken): the culmination. The far edge of the situation: completion, overreach, or letting go.
