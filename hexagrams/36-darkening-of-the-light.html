Darkening of the Light (明夷, Míng Yí) is hexagram 36 of the 64 hexagrams of the I Ching, in the traditional King Wen order. It is made of Earth (☷) above Fire (☲).

Meaning of hexagram 36

A dark time in which showing your brightness is risky. Preserve your inner light and stay quiet rather than fight what you cannot beat.

Advice

Keep your integrity private, avoid open confrontation and wait it out.

The two trigrams

Darkening of the Light places ☷ Earth (Kūn), yielding, receptive and nourishing, above ☲ Fire (Lí), bright, clinging and clear. The lower trigram is the inner side of the situation and the upper one its outer side.

The six lines of Darkening of the Light

A hexagram is read from the bottom line up. In a reading, a changing line (a 6 or a 9 in the three-coin method) draws attention to its position.

  1. First line (yang, solid): the beginning. The root of the matter: early stirrings, foundations, and what has not yet shown itself.
  2. Second line (yin, broken): the inner circle. Your immediate surroundings and steady supports: how things work at close range.
  3. Third line (yang, solid): the threshold. A point of tension between inner and outer, often a test or a restless push forward.
  4. Fourth line (yin, broken): the approach. Stepping into wider circles or nearer to authority; caution and good relationships matter here.
  5. Fifth line (yin, broken): the center of power. The place of influence and clear leadership: where the situation is most fully expressed.
  6. Sixth line (yin, broken): the culmination. The far edge of the situation: completion, overreach, or letting go.
